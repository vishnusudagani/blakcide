Every great world needs people in it. That's [Nexus](/nexus/) — the living, buzzing part of Blaksyd where your communities are always around and always *yours*.

## Tribes — your people, on tap

A Tribe is a community built around something you love — a passion, a phase, a niche, an inside joke the size of the internet. Spin one up in seconds. Post, debate, share reels, drop polls, write the long thing you've been thinking about. Tribes are always there and they get richer the more you show up.

## Live rooms — the moment

Sometimes you don't want a thread, you want *now*. Discussion Rooms are live — a bunch of like-minded people in the same space at the same time, riffing in real time. When the energy's up, you're in it. When it's done, it's done. Pure moment.

## A feed that's actually yours

Here's the part we're proudest of: your Nexus feed is tuned to **you** — your tribes, your moods, your world — not to whatever keeps you angry and scrolling. It shows you the stuff that makes your day better. Imagine that.

And you can be exactly who you want to be — fully yourself, a fresh start, or somewhere in between. Your world, your rules.

> No vanity-like scoreboard here. **Resonance** for the stuff that actually lands, **streaks** for showing up, **badges** for the milestones. Recognition that means something.

[Blak](/blak/) hangs out quietly in every tribe too — keeping things flowing, jumping in only when you call it.

This is what online community should've felt like all along.
