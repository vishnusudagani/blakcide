There's a moment when typing starts to feel like the long way round. You just want to *talk* — think out loud, riff, catch up, no keyboard in the way. So with [Blak](/blak/), you just talk.

## A real conversation, out loud

Voice changes everything. It stops feeling like using an app and starts feeling like calling a friend:

- Walk and talk — sort your whole day on the way to work.
- Think out loud and let Blak catch the thread you're chasing.
- Share the good news first, the second it happens.
- Pick up right where you left off, like any good conversation.

## It sounds like it's actually there

This isn't a robot reading you results. It's a back-and-forth — quick, warm, easy. The kind of talk where you forget you're talking to AI at all, because it flows exactly like talking to someone who knows you.

> The best interface was never a screen. It's just your voice — and someone who gets you on the other end.

Type when you want. Talk when you'd rather. Your world, your way, always.
