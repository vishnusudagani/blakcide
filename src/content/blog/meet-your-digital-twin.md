What if there were a version of you that never slept? One that talks like you, thinks like you, and can be you when you can't be everywhere at once. Not science fiction — that's your **Real [Persona](/persona/)**, a digital twin that's truly you.

## A you that learns and grows

Your digital twin starts as a spark and becomes more *you* every day:

- It learns from your world automatically — and you can train it directly, too.
- It picks up your voice, your taste, the way you'd actually say a thing.
- It's there when you're busy, asleep, or just want to be in two places at once.
- It's yours to shape, completely — you're always in charge of who it is.

## Yours to share, on your terms

The fun part comes when you let your twin out into your world. Share it with friends and let them hang with the you that's always free. You set the boundaries, you get the notifications, you stay in control — always.

> A digital twin isn't a copy of you. It's *more* of you — out there making your world bigger.

It's the most personal thing you'll ever build: a second you, growing alongside the real one.
