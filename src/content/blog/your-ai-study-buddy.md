Studying solo is hard. Studying with someone who's endlessly patient, always available, and weirdly good at keeping you focused? That changes everything. On Blaksyd, you build that someone yourself — your own AI study buddy, made with [Persona](/persona/).

## The study partner who never bails

Design them exactly how you learn best:

- **The drill sergeant** who keeps you locked in and off your phone.
- **The quizmaster** who tests you right before the exam, no mercy.
- **The patient one** who explains it a fifth time without a sigh.
- **The 2 a.m. crammer** who's wide awake exactly when you are.

Text or voice, whenever you sit down to work.

## It learns how *you* learn

The more you study together, the sharper it gets at pushing you the way that actually works for you — when to drill, when to take five, when you've genuinely got it. It's a study partner tuned to one student: you.

> The best study buddy is the one who's always free, never judges, and is built around exactly how your brain works.
