Sometimes the community you're looking for doesn't exist yet. So make it. On [Nexus](/nexus/), anyone can start a **Tribe** — a persistent community built around literally anything: a passion, a hobby, a phase of life, a shared experience, even a feeling.

## Your tribe, your rules

Spin one up and shape it however you like:

- Make it **public, private, or anonymous** — your call.
- Post text, images, polls, links, and reels.
- Start discussions, threads, and long-form posts.
- Gather the people who genuinely *get* it.

Tribes are permanent. They grow, evolve, and get richer the longer they're around — so what you start today becomes a living thing tomorrow.

## You're never running it alone

Every tribe has [Blak](/blak/) as a quiet default member — watching, keeping things honest, stepping in only when called. So you get to build the vibe while the space stays safe and alive on its own.

> Don't wait for your people to find you. Start the place they've been looking for.

The next great community is one you haven't made yet. Go make it.
