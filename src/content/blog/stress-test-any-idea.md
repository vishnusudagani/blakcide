Every big idea deserves a good grilling before you go all in. The problem is, the people around you are often too nice to give it. On Blaksyd, you build the perfect sparring partner — and then get a second opinion from the one who knows you best: you.

## First, the critic

Meet Sam — a Fantasy [Persona](/persona/) whose whole job is to tell it straight:

- Pokes holes in your plan, constructively.
- Asks the questions you've been avoiding.
- Pulls no punches — but always wants you to win.
- Never gets bored of going one more round.

Sam tears your idea apart so the real world doesn't have to.

## Then, a word with yourself

Once Sam's done, switch to your Real Persona and hear how *you* would approach it — your instincts, your past calls, your style of thinking, reflected back. Two perspectives, zero scheduling, total honesty.

> The best ideas aren't the ones nobody questioned. They're the ones that survived the questions.

Walk into the room already knowing where the weak spots are. That's an unfair advantage — and it's yours.
