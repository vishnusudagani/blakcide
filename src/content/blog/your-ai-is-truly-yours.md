The best things in your world are the ones that are truly *yours*. Your AI should be top of that list. On Blaksyd, [Blak](/blak/) and your [Persona](/persona/) are truly yours — and they stay that way.

## Private by default, always

Your world stays your world. That's not a setting you go hunting for — it's how Blaksyd works from the first hello:

- Your AI is shaped around you, for you — not packaged up for anyone else.
- You decide what's shared and what stays just between you and Blak.
- Share your Persona with friends on *your* terms, with the controls in your hands.
- When you set a boundary, it holds. No fine print, no surprises.

## Built to make your life better — full stop

Here's the simple version of our promise: most apps want your attention. Blaksyd wants your life to be better. That changes everything about how we treat what's yours — there's no game being played behind the curtain.

> Your world, your AI, your call. That's the whole deal.

When something is genuinely yours, you can relax into it. That's exactly how Blaksyd is meant to feel.
