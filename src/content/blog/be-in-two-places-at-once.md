Ever wished you could be in two places at once? Out at dinner *and* helping a friend plan the trip. Off living your life *and* still there for someone who wants your take. With [Persona](/persona/), you basically can.

## Your clone can hang out for you

Share your Real Persona with someone — say your friend Raj — and they can have real conversations with the digital you:

- Plan a trip together while you're busy with your day.
- Get your perspective on something, any hour.
- Just hang out with you — even when you're not online.

You're at dinner; Raj is planning your weekend with your Persona; you read it later and smile. That's a normal day on Blaksyd.

## You're always in control

Sharing a version of yourself only works if it's *safe*, so you hold the controls:

- Set boundaries on what your clone can and can't talk about.
- Get notified every single time your Persona is used.
- See every conversation it's had.

> Connection that doesn't need both people awake at the same time. That's the future of social.

Your world keeps moving — warmly, in your voice — even when you step away.
