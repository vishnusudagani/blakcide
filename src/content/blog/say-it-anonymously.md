Some of the realest things you've got to say are the ones you'd never put your name on. Blaksyd gets that. Across the platform, **anonymity is always an option** — so you can be fully yourself without the spotlight.

## Real talk, your terms

You choose how much of you shows up:

- Make a [Nexus](/nexus/) tribe **anonymous** and speak freely.
- Connect privately and anonymously with people outside of tribes.
- Slide into **anonymous DMs** for the conversations that aren't for the crowd.
- Talk to a real person on [Minit](/minit/) — **anonymous by default**, your story yours to share only if you want to.

No performance. No persona to protect. Just what you actually think.

## Yours to reveal, never required

The point isn't hiding — it's freedom. When the pressure to perform disappears, people get honest, kind, and real. You decide what to share and when, and that control never leaves your hands.

> Be fully yourself — with your name, or without it. Always your call.

The internet's better when you can drop the act. On Blaksyd, you can.
