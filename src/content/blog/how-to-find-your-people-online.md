The internet connected the whole planet and somehow still left a lot of people scrolling past everyone they'd actually click with. Finding *your* people — the ones who light up about the same things you do — should be the easy part. On Blaksyd, it is. That's [Nexus](/nexus/).

## Your corner, not the whole noisy room

Nexus is built so your people are always around:

- **Tribes** that stick around — real communities for the stuff you love, not a feed that forgets you tomorrow.
- **Live rooms** where the conversation is happening *right now*, and you can just walk in.
- **A feed tuned to you** — more of what you love, none of the outrage bait.
- **Anonymous DMs** when you want to reach out without the whole performance.

## Recognition that actually means something

Here you're seen for what you bring, not how loud you are. Nexus runs on **Resonance, streaks and badges** — real signals that you showed up and added something — instead of a pile of vanity likes.

> Your people are out there. Nexus is just the place where you finally find each other.

Less doomscrolling, more belonging. That's a corner of the internet worth coming back to.
