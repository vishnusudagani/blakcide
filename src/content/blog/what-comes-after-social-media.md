Be honest: social media got tired. Infinite scroll, outrage on tap, and a little counter of likes that somehow never feels like enough. It was built around *engagement* — not around you. So what comes next? Something that flips the whole thing on its head. We call it [Nexus](/nexus/).

## A world built around you

The next thing isn't a louder feed. It's a world that's actually yours:

- A feed tuned to **what you love**, not what makes you angry enough to keep scrolling.
- **Tribes** that last — real communities, not a timeline that forgets you by morning.
- **Live rooms** where things are happening now, with people who get it.
- Recognition through **Resonance**, streaks and badges — real signals you showed up and added something, not vanity likes.

## Your people, plus a digital you

Here's where it gets futuristic. Your [Persona](/persona/) — a digital you — can be out in your world even when you're off doing life. The next era of social isn't you performing for strangers. It's your people, your tribes, and even a version of you, all revolving around the real one.

> Social media wanted your attention. What comes next wants your world to be better.

The feed era is ending. Something a whole lot more *you* is almost here.
