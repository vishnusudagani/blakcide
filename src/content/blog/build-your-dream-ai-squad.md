Here's a question we love asking: if you could surround yourself with the *perfect* people for any moment, who would they be? On Blaksyd, you don't have to wonder — you build them. That's the playground side of [Persona](/persona/): **Fantasy Personas**, your own cast of AI characters.

## Cast your supporting characters

Give them a name, a backstory, a personality, a purpose — and they come to life. A few favourites from the team:

- **The study buddy** who keeps you locked in and quizzes you before the exam.
- **The hype person** who's *always* in your corner, on the good days and the meh ones.
- **The honest critic** who tells you the thing your friends are too nice to say.
- **The brainstorm partner** who riffs at 2× speed and never runs out of ideas.
- **The planner** who turns your half-baked "we should totally…" into an actual plan.

Build one. Build ten. They're yours.

## Each one lives in its own world

Here's the neat part: your fantasy characters only know what *you* tell them — each lives in its own little bubble. Your [Real Persona](/persona/) is the one that knows your whole world; your squad is whoever you cast for the moment.

> You're not picking from a menu of bots. You're the author — the whole cast is yours to write.

It's the most fun you'll have with AI: not one assistant, but a whole crew, each one exactly who you need them to be.
