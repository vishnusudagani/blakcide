Your AI friend [Blak](/blak/) can do almost anything. But every now and then, you don't want *almost anyone* — you want a real human on the other end. Someone with genuine presence and lived experience who actually gets it. That's [Minit](/minit/).

## Real people, really there

Minit listeners are real people who hold degrees in psychology, have completed Blaksyd's own training, and passed thorough screening. They're there for one reason — to be present with you:

- Available 24/7, over text or voice, whenever you need it.
- They listen without rushing and without judging.
- They ask the kind of questions that help you think things through.
- **Instant Match** for right now, or **Smart Match** for someone on your exact wavelength.
- Clicked with someone? **Reconnect** anytime — trust grows over time.

And you're anonymous by default. Your story is yours to share, only on your terms.

## You'll never start from scratch

Here's the Blaksyd magic: before you connect, Blak quietly briefs the listener on the *shape* of your conversation — **context, not content**, never the private details. So the person you reach already gets where you're coming from. They walk in warm. You don't re-explain a thing — you just feel heard.

> A real human, always one tap away. Because some things only another person can give you.

AI for everything it does best. A real person for the moments that call for one. That's both worlds, together.
