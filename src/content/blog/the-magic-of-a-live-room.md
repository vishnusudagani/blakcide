There's a special kind of energy in a conversation that's happening *right now* — everyone present, ideas flying, nobody scrolling yesterday's takes. That's a **Discussion Room** on [Nexus](/nexus/): a live moment you can just walk into.

## Live while it's alive

Unlike a Tribe (which is always there), a Discussion Room is a *moment*:

- Real-time, with like-minded people gathered at once.
- Electric while the energy's up — and it ends when the energy does.
- Can live inside a tribe, or stand on its own.
- Open to whoever wants to be part of it.

No replays to catch up on. No pressure to perform. Just the buzz of being there.

## Show up and you're in

The best part is how easy it is — no green room, no setup. You see a room is live, you step in, you're part of it. [Blak](/blak/) keeps the space honest in the background, so the vibe stays good.

> Some moments you had to be there for. Nexus makes sure you can be.

When the conversation is alive, you don't want to read about it later. You want to be in the room.
