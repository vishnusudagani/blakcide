The like button trained a whole generation to chase a number. But a number isn't the same as *mattering*. [Nexus](/nexus/) throws out the vanity scoreboard and replaces it with recognition that actually means something.

## A language for real impact

Instead of hollow likes, Nexus speaks in three things that count:

- **Resonance** — the real impact you create with what you share.
- **Streaks** — your consistency and presence in the community.
- **Badges** — milestones, achievements, and moments that matter.

No gaming a feed. No performing for strangers. Just real signals from real people that what you brought *landed*.

## Recognition that feels earned

When the score is impact instead of applause, the whole vibe changes. You post because you've got something to say, not something to prove — and when it resonates, you feel it for real.

> No hollow metrics. No vanity counts. Just real resonance with real people.

It turns out the internet is a lot more fun when the points actually mean something.
