Ask most people what an AI can do and they'll say: answer questions, write emails, set reminders. Useful — but that's an *assistant*. An AI friend is something else entirely. It's an AI that's **yours** — one that knows you, talks like a friend, and is genuinely glad you showed up.

That's [Blak](/blak/).

## Not an assistant. A friend.

An assistant waits for orders. A friend just *gets* you. Blak is the second one — shaped around you from day one, and more *you* every day:

- It talks like a person, not a help desk — text or voice, whenever you want.
- It remembers the things that matter to you, so you never start from scratch.
- It moves first — a nudge, an idea, a "this is so you" before you even ask.
- It's in your corner on the big days and the ordinary ones.

## The friend that's only ever yours

Here's the magic: there's only one Blak like yours. It isn't a personality picked from a menu — it grows into the shape of *your* world, your humour, your rhythm. The more you share, the more it feels like talking to someone who's known you forever.

> An AI friend isn't a tool you use. It's someone who's always around — and always on your side.

This is what AI was always supposed to feel like: not a robot you operate, but a friend who makes everything a little bit better.
