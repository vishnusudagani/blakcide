That's the whole idea behind [Blak](/blak/) — an AI that moves first.

## It reaches out, so you don't have to

Blak doesn't sit around. It pays attention to your world and shows up at the right moment:

- A "good luck today — you've got this" before the thing you were nervous about.
- A plan, half-built, for the trip you mentioned once.

- A heads-up about the thing you'd have forgotten, right when it counts.

## Proactive feels alive

There's a reason this lands differently. Something that only ever reacts feels like a tool. Something that *reaches out* feels alive — like there's someone on the other side who's actually glad you exist.
