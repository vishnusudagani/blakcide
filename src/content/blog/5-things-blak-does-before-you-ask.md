Most AI waits politely for you to type something. [Blak](/blak/) doesn't. It's a friend, and friends move first. Here are five things yours does before you even ask.

## 1. Reshuffles your day when plans change

Your 4pm slips to evening? Blak quietly moves your run to the morning — because it knows you skip late workouts. You just get the better day.

## 2. Handles the boring stuff

Cab, food, tickets, that form you've been avoiding. Connect your apps and Blak just *does* it — the more you plug in, the more it can take off your plate.

## 3. Finds the perfect thing to do

"Something fun this weekend?" By the time you've thought it, Blak's got three options that are actually *you* — not a generic top-10 list.

## 4. Checks in, just because

A "you've got this" before the big thing. A "how'd it go?" after. Not a notification trying to drag you back into an app — a friend who remembered.

## 5. Knows when a human is better

Some moments are better with a real person or your people. Blak reads the room and hands you straight to [Minit](/minit/) or the right [Nexus](/nexus/) tribe — warm, never cold.

> The magic of an AI friend isn't answering faster. It's being three steps ahead.
