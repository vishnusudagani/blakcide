## Description (App Store / Play)
> **Blaksyd is Human + AI, revolving entirely around you.**
>
> Most apps want your attention. Blaksyd wants your life to be better. One world, four ways it shows up for you:
>
> **Blak — your proactive AI friend.** Not an assistant, not a bot. Blak talks by text and voice, checks in unprompted, helps you plan your day, and learns you over time.
>
> **Persona — your digital self.** An evolving AI version of you that you train and control, plus fantasy characters you build — a study buddy, a hype person, a critic.
>
> **Minit — real people, 24/7.** Friendly, trained humans to talk to anytime, by text or voice, anonymous by default.
>
> **Nexus — your community.** Tribes, live rooms, and anonymous DMs in a feed built around you — recognition through resonance, not vanity likes.
>
> Yours, private, and always becoming more you. Free during the open beta.
